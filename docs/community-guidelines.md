# Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 21, 2026

SkateU is a campus skate-spot map. The point is to help skaters find spots, share what they know, and not wreck the session for everyone else.

These guidelines apply to usernames, spots, photos, comments, replies, likes, and removal requests. They work together with the Terms of Use. If you do not want to follow them, SkateU is not the right place.

SkateU accounts are for people 13 and older. Do not create an account for someone under 13. Do not use SkateU to identify or target minors.

Questions or reports that are not a spot removal request: [support@skateu.app](mailto:support@skateu.app).

## Keep it appropriate

Do not post sexual content, including nudity or sexually suggestive material. That ban applies to everyone.

Do not post other inappropriate content, including graphic violence and illegal activity.

## Respect other skaters

Talk to people like you are on the same campus, because you probably are.

Do not harass, bully, threaten, or pile on anyone. Do not post hate, slurs, or content that attacks people for who they are.

Disagreements about a spot are fine. Personal attacks are not.

## Keep it real

Do not intentionally submit fake spots.

Do not intentionally submit misleading spot information, including a pin dropped in the wrong place on purpose.

If a spot is gone, private, or a duplicate, use Request Removal instead of starting a fight in the comments.

## Share spots you actually know

Add spots you have skated or can describe honestly. Use a clear name, a useful description, and photos that show the spot.

Spots you post are public. SkateU may feature them, including photos, on our Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

Do not upload photos you do not have the right to share.

Do not post photos or comments that expose someone else’s private personal information, such as a phone number, home address, or private document.

## Do not spam or impersonate people

Do not flood SkateU with junk, ads, unrelated links, or repeated low-effort posts.

Do not impersonate another skater, a school, or SkateU.

Do not use a username that pretends you are staff, official, or someone else.

## Do not use SkateU to do harm

SkateU is for finding and sharing skate spots. It is not for planning trespass, vandalism, or anything illegal.

Do not post illegal content. Do not post content that is meant to help someone get hurt.

A spot in the app is not permission to skate there. Respect school rules, private property, and posted signs.

## Do not abuse removal requests

Request Removal is for spots that should not stay up: gone, private or restricted, in the wrong place, dangerous, duplicate, or similar problems.

Do not use it to harass a skater, settle a score, or spam the review process.

Do not abuse comment reports or blocking. Those tools are for keeping SkateU usable, not for silencing people you disagree with about a spot.

## How to report a problem

- **Spots:** open the spot on the map and use Request Removal. You will pick a reason and can add details.
- **Comments:** open the comment and use Report. You will pick a reason and can add details.
- **A skater:** use Block on their comment or spot. You will stop seeing their spots and comments. You can unblock them in Settings.
- **Anything else,** including a photo, username, or behavior that is not covered above: email [support@skateu.app](mailto:support@skateu.app) and tell us where it is.

We may not be able to reply to every message, but we use reports to keep SkateU in shape.

## What SkateU may do

SkateU may hide or remove content, limit features, or suspend or close an account that breaks these guidelines or the Terms of Use.

Some content is checked automatically before it is posted. That does not mean everything that gets through is approved forever.

If we take action, it is to keep the map useful and the community from turning into a mess. We will not always send a warning first.
